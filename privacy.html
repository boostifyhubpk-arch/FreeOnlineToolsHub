YOUR DATA MATTERS

Privacy information

A plain-language overview of how this starter website handles data.

Last updated: October 2026

Browser processing

Image tools, text tools, calculators, password generation and color tools are designed to process inputs in your browser. The website does not include an account system or a first-party database in this starter version.

Third-party services

The QR Code Generator sends the text or URL you enter to the third-party QR image service at api.qrserver.com to generate the QR image. Do not enter confidential, personal or sensitive information into that tool. The third-party service may process technical request data under its own policies.

Hosting and logs

This website may be hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses in server logs according to its own terms and privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

No advertising or analytics code is included in this starter package. If you add advertising, analytics, contact forms or other third-party services later, update this policy to describe what those services collect and how they use data.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details on the Contact page after replacing the placeholder instructions with a real contact method.
