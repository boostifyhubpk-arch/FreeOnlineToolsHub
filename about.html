ABOUT SIMPLEKIT

Useful tools, without the fuss.

SimpleKit is a small collection of free browser-based text tools for everyday writing tasks.

What the tools do

The current version includes a word and character counter, a case converter, and an extra-space cleaner. These tools run in your browser and do not need an account.

Accuracy and feedback

Tools are provided as-is. Please review important text and results before relying on them. For corrections or suggestions, use the contact details on the Contact page.
