ABOUT SIMPLEKIT

Useful tools, without the fuss.

SimpleKit is a collection of free browser-based tools for everyday text, image, QR, and unit-conversion tasks.

Current tools

The site includes a word and character counter, case converter, extra-space cleaner, image compressor, QR code generator, and unit converter. Most processing happens in your browser. QR generation uses an external service and sends the entered text or URL to that provider.

Accuracy

Tools are provided as-is. Check important outputs before relying on them.
