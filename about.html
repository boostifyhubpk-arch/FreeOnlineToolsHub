OUR PURPOSE

Useful tools.
Less friction.

FreeOnlineToolsHub is a collection of straightforward browser utilities for everyday digital tasks.

Our approach

We aim to make common tasks easier with focused tools, clear instructions and a simple interface. The site is a starter project and tools may improve over time.

Privacy by design

Many tools process input in your browser. However, some features may use third-party services. For example, the QR code generator sends the entered text or URL to a QR service to create its image. Read our Privacy page before using the site with sensitive information.

Accuracy

These tools are provided for general convenience. Please verify important calculations, conversions and outputs before relying on them for business, legal, financial or other consequential decisions.
